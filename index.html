Profile

Physics-based modeling, optimization and data science

Born a physicist, I am passionate about building models to understand how things work. At YPF-Tecnología I led multi-disciplinary teams that turned business problems into models and measurement instruments to optimize field operations, and I was program leader for the R&D team on intelligent tools and equipment — work that resulted in patents, awards, new industrial products and numerous publications. In Dec 2021 I joined Mercado Libre as an Expert in the Shipping Optimization Team, where I was promoted to Sr Expert and then to the Sr Manager role, responsible for the development of all of Meli's optimization algorithms across its Fulfillment Centers in LATAM.
